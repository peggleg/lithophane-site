# Weekly pill box

Bathroom collection item `pill`, available at `/bathroom/?item=pill`. Seven fixed compartments in a single base, with individual MON–SUN lids. Lift-off lids remain the default; an optional hinged version has a separate hinge for each day. Default assembled dimensions 168 × 65 × 26 mm; usable straight spans per day approximately 21.1 × 59.1 × 18.7 mm below the lid guides. Rounded corners reduce capacity locally. Width/depth resize the wells and lids together; usable width must remain at least 18 mm per day, depth 20 mm, overall height 18–60 mm. All parts retain the 170 mm A1 mini design allowance. Six shared surface styles decorate the base.

In the lift-off version, each lid has a 1.2 mm guide ring entering 2.5 mm, with the selected 0.2–0.8 mm clearance per side. It rests on the rim; this is not a locking catch. Individual finger scallops expose the edges for removal. Print base bottom-down, lids top-down with rings upward. Text is engraved 0.6 mm into the top, with its orientation compensated for the final assembly rotation. No hinges or glue. Print one lid first and check its fit before printing the set. Clearances, wear and retention need physical testing.

Not airtight or child-resistant. Follow the medicine packaging storage instructions; keep away from children and bathroom moisture. This is an organiser, not a substitute for packaging required to protect particular medicines.

The profile is unchanged: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, A1 mini/Generic PLA/textured PEI, 8% gyroid, two wall loops, three top and bottom layers, zero minimum skin thickness. Guides, estimates, 3MF and STL sidecars use kind `pill`. The exporter includes eight named parts for lift-off lids for either lid style. Colours are previews only.

Validation: `test/pill-box.test.js` checks all styles and clearances, connected parts, closed floors, seven enclosed wells, independent lid removal, bounds, text orientation and profile/guide consistency. The Home collection tests also include this item. `scripts/check-pill-box-browser.mjs` exercises desktop/mobile generation, actual downloads, profiles, invalid sizes and reset. Actual Bambu Studio 2.8.2.61 import, desktop Open Project/Save Project and representative native slices verify the export profile beyond archive inspection. Printed moisture resistance, retention and physical fit are not validated.

## Individually clip-on hinged lids

Choose **Individual clip-on hinged lids**. Each lid has two C clips snapping vertically onto integral 3 mm axles. No loose pins or glue. The 2.4 mm clip throat has a flared lead-in; the selected clearance sets the bearing gap, capped at 0.35 mm radially to preserve clip wall thickness. Rear hinge allowance remains 8 mm. Base prints bottom-down, lids top-down. One base and seven lids; filename suffix `-clip-hinged`. Preview opens each selected day to 110 degrees, without a physical travel stop.

Enable **Single-day clip hinge test print** to export just the Monday well and lid. The base is cropped to one well, then its exterior is decorated on all sides (including the newly exposed end); the lid is identical: dimensions, finish, clearance, print orientation and profiles match. Download 3MF or STL ZIP. Print the same file in PLA and PETG, choosing the correct filament preset/temperatures in the slicer; the supplied project starts with Generic PLA. Keep 8% gyroid, two walls and 3/3 skins. Cool fully, snap evenly from above, compare insertion force, smooth rotation, retention after repeated use, and cracks/whitening. Do not force a tight clip. Physical fit and fatigue await these prints. Test filenames use `-clip-test`.

Geometry tests check connected parts, closed wells, pairwise assembled clearances and independent lid motion over the supported dimensions/styles. The test sample is checked against the full design. Both printers retain their matching project profiles.
