# Individual drawer surface styles

Drawer units offer matching or mixed styles. In mixed mode, each row/column position can choose one of the six existing surface styles, or inherit the shared style. Detail depth/spacing, openwork, fit clearance and the 8% gyroid / two-wall / 3+3 profile remain shared. This controls printed geometry, not filament colour assignment.

Each drawer gets its own body depth and front position based on its style's projection. Finished fronts remain aligned and backs clear the case. Rear-connector allowance is applied to every drawer. Colour sheets and their near-flush retaining guides are generated from that individual drawer's final dimensions. Reset restores matching styles. The guide lists every drawer's actual style; the size list includes it and mixed-style filenames are marked.

Validation uses all six styles together in a 3-by-2 grid, with/without rear connectors and with/without near-flush colour sheets, checking connected solids, front extents, drawer insertion and sheet clearances. Physical fit and finish still need print testing.
