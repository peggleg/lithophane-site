PRINTABLE PARTS + FIT TESTS—

Start small: print switch-plate-fit-test and keycap-socket-fit-test. Check clipping, boxed-stem clearance and full switch travel before making the whole clicker. Do not force a tight cap.

FROM FIRST FIT TO FIRST CLICK

A few small parts. A lot of little clicks.

Test the fit

Use your actual switches and printer. Adjust the plate opening, socket and insertion depth if needed. Nothing is physically fit-tested yet.

Print the parts

The open-bottom base prints top-face down. Keycaps export socket-down and need supports below their overhanging undersides; keep sockets clear. Read the supplied guide and inspect the sliced parts.

Put it together

Clip switches into the top plate and gently add the caps. Check free movement and that metal pins stay recessed inside the base. No wiring, battery or electronics needed.
