# Monstera coaster outline

The earlier shape was a rounded blade with three straight repeating slots per side. The revised original cubic drawing uses a pointed cordate blade, curved basal lobes, a short curved petiole, uneven rounded edge lobes and deep marginal splits. Seven teardrop-shaped inner fenestrations vary in size, position and angle.

Botanical reference: [University of Minnesota Extension — Monstera deliciosa](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/propagating-monstera-deliciosa), describing mature heart-shaped, perforated, deeply lobed leaves. No reference photograph is traced or shipped.

Both backing options share the lobed silhouette. The open version cuts the inner holes through; the backed version retains a continuous floor beneath them. Recessed coasters retain their perimeter rim. A shared transform normalizes the drawing to the exact requested width/depth; holders use that same outline. The profile remains 8% gyroid, 2 walls and 5/5 skin layers for coasters, 3/3 for holders.

Validation includes all surfaces, backing choices and 4–6 counts, holder insertion/rack clearances, actual part limits, all holder finishes, seven enclosed holes and a connected 0.8 mm inset core at 60 × 60, 60 × 160, 160 × 60, 100 × 100 and 160 × 160 mm. This verifies nominal geometry; printed fit and moisture resistance require physical tests.

## Hanging leaves on a branching plant

The reference calls for leaves hanging from a plant, rather than individual posts standing in a pot. The holder now has **one central trunk**, two integral branch hubs and separate curved branches. Numbered keyed trunk sections keep every individual print within 170 mm. One keyed socket in the pot's lift-out plate anchors the trunk. Surface styles apply to the pot as before.

Branches print flat, 1.8 mm thick, with rectangular keys fitting the hubs. Their upward-facing pegs pass through a **3.2 mm hanging hole in the leaf's reinforced stem**. Leaves hang downward at 60 degrees from horizontal; lift up and outward to remove them. This is a gravity-seated hanging connection, not a friction clamp. Assemble the trunk in number order, add lower branches then upper branches, and hang the lower leaves before the upper tier. Two height levels prevent leaf collisions. Physical stability, peg strength, joint fit and retention still need print testing.

Monstera coasters now offer a hanging-hole checkbox, enabled by default and required for the plant display. It can be switched off for ordinary coasters. The reinforced round stem pad and neck remain inside the selected width/depth. The hanging hole is outside the main leaf blade and intentionally passes through even backed coasters; the seven inner decorative fenestrations retain the backed/open choice. Keep this option enabled when exporting coasters for the new plant. Previous coasters without the stem hole do not hang on the new pegs.

The fuller heart-shaped base, elongated tip and curved engraved veins remain. Veins are recessed up to 0.4 mm, retaining 2.4 mm beneath them; engraving is omitted at the minimum floor thickness. The backing over the main blade remains continuous in backed mode, except for the separate stem hanging hole. Suggested leaf proportions are 1:1.25; both dimensions stay adjustable.

Reference leaves appear only in the assembled holder preview. They are excluded from holder downloads, part counts and filament estimates. Download the matched coaster set separately: leaves retain 5/5 skins, every holder part retains 3/3, and both retain 8% gyroid and two walls. Preview colours do not assign filament. Inspect trunk-joint bridges and long-part adhesion; brim and support settings are not silently changed.

Validation covers 4–6 leaves, extreme 60–160 mm dimensions, all pot styles, two fit clearances, connected parts, keyed trunk/branch insertion, upward leaf removal, all final assembly intersections, optional hanging holes, backing and holder fit. The assembled plant may exceed the printer volume; each printable part stays within the A1 mini design limit.

## Approved plant-pot redesign

The planter now has a taller tapered silhouette, a rounded support-friendly foot, a rolled lip and a recessed dark soil-like deck, retaining the central branching plant. Pot diameter follows coaster width (80–140 mm) and height is 78% of diameter; the default is about 105 × 82 mm. All six surface styles follow the taper. Warm ivory is the default plant palette. The deck sits 5 mm below the top, on an integral annular ledge. Shallow dimples texture its soil face.

The socket is underneath the deck. Print the deck TOP-DOWN (soil face on the bed, socket up); the pot remains bottom-down. Inspect the small blind-socket roof bridge. Insert the deck with the socket underneath, then fit the central trunk. Remove the trunk before lifting the deck for access. The 8 mm trunk keys and existing branch/leaf fits are unchanged, but pot, deck and trunk heights must be regenerated together; previous assemblies do not interchange. Both printer profiles retain 8% gyroid, two walls, 3/3 holder skins; separate leaf coasters retain 5/5. Colours are preview choices; assign filament in your slicer.

Digital tests cover connected parts, all surface styles, 4–6 hanging leaves, insertion/removal, assembled intersections and estimated load centres compared with the smaller actual ground-contact footprint. These checks do not establish physical tip resistance, connector strength or retention; test the printed set on a stable surface.
