# Weekly pill box

Bathroom collection item `pill`, available at `/bathroom/?item=pill`. Seven fixed compartments in a single base, with individual MON–SUN lids. Lift-off lids remain the default; an optional hinged version has a separate hinge for each day. Default assembled dimensions 168 × 65 × 26 mm; usable straight spans per day approximately 21.1 × 59.1 × 18.7 mm below the lid guides. Rounded corners reduce capacity locally. Width/depth resize the wells and lids together; usable width must remain at least 18 mm per day, depth 20 mm, overall height 18–60 mm. All parts retain the 170 mm A1 mini design allowance. Six shared surface styles decorate the base.

In the lift-off version, each lid has a 1.2 mm guide ring entering 2.5 mm, with the selected 0.2–0.8 mm clearance per side. It rests on the rim; this is not a locking catch. Individual finger scallops expose the edges for removal. Print base bottom-down, lids top-down with rings upward. Text is engraved 0.6 mm into the top, with its orientation compensated for the final assembly rotation. No hinges or glue. Print one lid first and check its fit before printing the set. Clearances, wear and retention need physical testing.

Not airtight or child-resistant. Follow the medicine packaging storage instructions; keep away from children and bathroom moisture. This is an organiser, not a substitute for packaging required to protect particular medicines.

The profile is unchanged: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, A1 mini/Generic PLA/textured PEI, 8% gyroid, two wall loops, three top and bottom layers, zero minimum skin thickness. Guides, estimates, 3MF and STL sidecars use kind `pill`. The exporter includes eight named parts for lift-off lids or fifteen for hinged lids. Colours are previews only.

Validation: `test/pill-box.test.js` checks all styles and clearances, connected parts, closed floors, seven enclosed wells, independent lid removal, bounds, text orientation and profile/guide consistency. The Home collection tests also include this item. `scripts/check-pill-box-browser.mjs` exercises desktop/mobile generation, actual downloads, profiles, invalid sizes and reset. Actual Bambu Studio 2.8.2.61 import, desktop Open Project/Save Project and representative native slices verify the export profile beyond archive inspection. Printed moisture resistance, retention and physical fit are not validated.

## Individually hinged lids

Choose **Individual hinged lids** in the Bathroom pill-box controls. Every day has two integral base knuckles, one integral lid knuckle and its own separate 2 mm printed pin. The back hinge allowance takes 8 mm from the chosen overall depth; width/depth/height remain resizable and every finished part stays inside the 170 mm allowance. The set contains one base, seven lids and seven pins. Hinged filenames include `-hinged`.

Print the base bottom-down, lids top-down and pins head-down. Bore roofs are teardrops in each supplied printing orientation. Rear base arms use tapered support roots. Align each lid between its matching base knuckles, insert the pin from the side, then secure only the pin head to the stationary outside barrel with a tiny dab of adhesive. Keep the shaft and moving central knuckle free of glue. Test one hinge first; pins are replaceable. Hinged lids sit on the rim without locking catches or the lift-off version's deep guide rings. There is no physical opening stop. Preview opens to 110 degrees, for all days together or one selected day while its neighbours remain closed.

Validation adds six surface styles, minimum-height/thick-wall and maximum-depth cases, connected barrels/pins, pairwise assembled clearances, and individual Mon/Thu/Sun swing checks at 5/30/60/90/110 degrees. The browser test switches between both lid types, checks independent day previews, downloads both variants, and checks resize errors/mobile layouts. Native Bambu import/slicing covers the hinged base, lid and pin; desktop Open Project/Save Project verifies the actual generated hinged download. The shared 8% gyroid, 2 walls, 3/3 skins policy remains unchanged. Physical hinge fit, adhesive retention and print quality still require a test print.

The native single-pin check also exercises 3MF XML escaping for the `less than 0.1 g` weight estimate. Reserved characters in object names and metadata are escaped; the shared print profile is unchanged.
