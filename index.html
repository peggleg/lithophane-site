FROM IDEA TO SOMETHING YOU CAN HOLD

Three steps. Endless possibilities.

01

Pick a project

Start with something useful, something playful or something personal.

02

Make it fit

Choose dimensions, lettering and styles. Explore your actual model in the preview.

03

Make it real

Download your print files, open the project in your slicer and follow its assembly guide.

Each part is checked against your selected printer size. A1 mini is the default, with a 170 mm design allowance; choose Bambu A1 for the 256 mm build volume. Print profiles follow each design, including reinforced mounts and extra coaster surface layers.
