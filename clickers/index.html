FROM FIRST FIT TO FIRST CLICK

A few small parts. A lot of little clicks.

Test the fit

Use your actual switches and printer. Adjust the plate opening, socket and insertion depth if needed. Nothing is physically fit-tested yet.

Print the parts

Print the tray flat on its solid bottom and the deck flat alongside it. Keycaps export socket-down and need supports below their overhanging undersides; keep sockets clear. Read the supplied guide and inspect the sliced parts.

Put it together

Clip switches into the separate deck, press the deck gently into its ledge and add the caps. The closed tray encloses the pins. Check deck retention and free movement. No wiring, battery or electronics needed.
