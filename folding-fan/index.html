03 / FROM PARTS TO FAN

Print. Assemble. Unfold.

1. Print a pivot test

Check the printed pin, coarse-thread nut and three test eyelets for fit and movement. Clearances are provisional.

2. Print your fan

Start with white PLA, flat backs on the bed and 0.2 mm layers. Inspect thin image areas in your slicer. Test three blades before the complete assembly.

3. Assemble and adjust

Stack blades in number order between the guards, fit washers and gently tighten the printed nut. Attach ribbon behind the ribs while open to set the opening limit.

Prototype design: verify image transmission, interference, pivot durability and stand stability. A 1.2 mm frame is six layers at 0.2 mm; image regions vary in thickness.
