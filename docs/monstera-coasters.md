# Monstera coaster outline

The earlier shape was a rounded blade with three straight repeating slots per side. The revised original cubic drawing uses a pointed cordate blade, curved basal lobes, a short curved petiole, uneven rounded edge lobes and deep marginal splits. Seven teardrop-shaped inner fenestrations vary in size, position and angle.

Botanical reference: [University of Minnesota Extension — Monstera deliciosa](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/propagating-monstera-deliciosa), describing mature heart-shaped, perforated, deeply lobed leaves. No reference photograph is traced or shipped.

Both backing options share the lobed silhouette. The open version cuts the inner holes through; the backed version retains a continuous floor beneath them. Recessed coasters retain their perimeter rim. A shared transform normalizes the drawing to the exact requested width/depth; holders use that same outline. The profile remains 8% gyroid, 2 walls and 5/5 skin layers for coasters, 3/3 for holders.

Validation includes all surfaces, backing choices and 4–6 counts, holder insertion/rack clearances, actual part limits, all holder finishes, seven enclosed holes and a connected 0.8 mm inset core at 60 × 60, 60 × 160, 160 × 60, 100 × 100 and 160 × 160 mm. This verifies nominal geometry; printed fit and moisture resistance require physical tests.

## Veined leaf and plant display

The user's leaf-coaster plant reference leads to a fuller heart-shaped base, longer tapered point and the missing visual detail: a curved midrib and eight secondary veins. These are engraved up to 0.4 mm deep rather than raised under the glass. Engraving adapts to preserve 2.4 mm beneath it; at the minimum floor thickness it is omitted and the UI/guide explains how to restore it. Selecting monstera from equal width/depth suggests a 1:1.25 leaf proportion; both dimensions remain adjustable. Backed/open details and flat/recessed surfaces remain available.

The new **Monstera plant display** sits alongside the existing stacking holder and upright rack. It includes one styled pot, a lift-out keyed socket plate and 4–6 removable stems with angled, open-front petiole cradles. Leaf references show the complete plant but are excluded from holder STL/3MF, part counts and filament estimates. Download the matching coaster set separately: leaves retain 5/5 skins, all holder parts 3/3, with 8% gyroid and 2 walls throughout.

The plate notch identifies the first socket. Its guide lists counter-clockwise socket angles for the named stems. Fit lower-tier leaves before the upper tier. Two vertical levels keep leaves apart; the leaf angle is reduced automatically for deep leaves so every stem stays within the 170 mm limit. Pot and collar dimensions derive from coaster width, thickness and set count. A complete assembly can exceed the bed dimensions; every exported part fits individually. Surface styles apply to the pot. Preview colours do not assign filament or colour changes. Inspect long stems, cradle overhangs/bridges, adhesion and any need for brim/local supports in the slicer; these are not enabled automatically.

Digital tests cover closed connected parts, 4–6 leaves, extreme 60–160 mm sizes, two clearance limits, all pot styles, leaf insertion in assembly order, socket/plate insertion and all final part/reference intersections. Physical stability, socket fit, cradle retention and wear still need a test print.
