# Modular cupboards and drawer connectors

The Desk collection has a cupboard alternative to drawers. Closed assembled width, including hinge hardware, is limited to 170 mm. Select 1–3 modules high and 1–2 columns across within that width. Each column needs at least 70 mm. Module height is 80–170 mm and depth 60–170 mm. Two 170 mm modules give a 340 mm cupboard. Every finished printable part is separately checked within 170 mm; assemblies may exceed the printer volume.

Floors and tops default to automatic: one floor below and one top above each vertical column, without intermediate floors. Each can instead be included in every module or omitted everywhere. Cupboards use single doors only and retain a full-width interior without a centre divider. Front stiles and optional magnet mounts have tapered support webs. Back walls and sides remain integral.

Single doors offer left or right hinges and close flush with the front frame. The printed-m5-v1 layout replaces the failed integral barrels with independent two-piece hinges, bolted to reinforced case/door pads. Each knuckle prints axis-up directly on the bed. The case half carries an opening stop; the moving half bolts behind the door. Countersunk round heads sit flush, with screwdriver slots and an included printed flat driver. Case mounting holes and head seats have roofs appropriate to the back-down printing orientation. Matching captive nuts stop the mounting nuts turning.

All fasteners are printed: nominal 5 mm diameter with a custom coarse 2 mm pitch, not ISO M5. Four mounting bolt/nut pairs and one smooth pivot bolt/nut serve each hinge. Only the pivot end is threaded. Modules below 150 mm use one hinge, taller modules two. The shortened pivot withdraws downward after removing its nut, including in an 80 mm-high connected stack. Assemble hardware before connecting cases where side access would be blocked. Regenerate cases, doors and hardware together; old integral-hinge parts are incompatible.

The optional M5 hinge test includes 15 parts: one complete two-piece hinge, its mounting and pivot hardware, a driver, and actual cropped case/door mounting sections. Case strips keep the selected full depth and back-down orientation; separate hinge halves print directly on the bed. The same test can be printed in PLA and PETG by selecting the matching filament preset.
Vertical door sections join into one moving leaf using two rear splice keys at every seam. Closed-ended dovetail tunnels retain each key; assemble the panels flat before presenting the whole door to the case and bolting on its hinges. Case ports connect with removable split-stud snap clips with long in-plane spring arms pressed from the rear. Their retaining heads sit inside the cases; squeeze studs from inside to release. Internal grid joins are generated automatically. Additional top/bottom and side ports, plus spare keys, allow later connections. Rear clips project 2.4 mm beyond the requested body depth. Spare keys are included in downloads/gram estimates and shown only in printing-orientations view.

Stops allow about 110° on each door. The preview uses the actual per-door axis/limit and moves panels, pulls and splice keys together. Flex catches and matching receivers are integral. Separate printed pulls use rigid quarter-turn T-studs. Insert with the head aligned to the slot, then turn the face groove vertical to retain; turn back to horizontal to remove. Optional paired 6.2 mm pockets accept user-supplied nominal 6 × 2 mm magnets; check polarity/fit before gluing.

Door surface styles and openwork use the shared style names. Pattern fields retain a solid edge frame, hinge/handle/catch surrounds and solid splice regions. Thin face grooves, enclosed splice channels and small horizontal bores still need slicer toolpath/bridge inspection; supports are not silently enabled.

Drawer units optionally gain vertical, side-by-side or both sets of rear connector ports and printed snap clips. Drawer bodies shorten by 2.4 mm to clear stud heads. Existing feet/pockets are retained: use locating-pocket units below and a smooth-top final unit above. Vertical pitch is unit height minus 1.2 mm. Match width, depth, row count, wall and connector settings. Remove drawers to release clips. Defaults without connectors retain the original drawer geometry.

Cases, doors and ordinary parts retain 8% gyroid, two wall loops and three top/bottom layers. Separate hinge halves, bolts, nuts and driver use per-object 30% gyroid, four walls and five top/bottom layers. Minimum skin thickness remains zero. 3MF object metadata and PART-PRINT-SETTINGS.json carry the hardware overrides. Filament estimates, guides, sidecars and recognised Bambu projects use the shared profile. No photo/coaster/keyring/nameplate profile changes.

Validation covers connected closed meshes, all style/openwork combinations, automatic/manual faces, left/right single doors, closed width/part limits, rigid opening motion after catch release, physical stop engagement, smallest-stack M5 pivot removal, joined door/case alignment, magnet pads, snap-clip seated fits and drawer travel with actual clip heads. Snap engagement and flex catches intentionally need elastic deflection and require printed tests; digital rigid checks cannot prove retention, wear or strength. These are small desktop storage assemblies, not load-rated furniture. Test one hinge, connector and module before a large stack.

## Optional storage details

Every new feature defaults off. Single left/right-hinged doors remain available with these options off.

- Fixed shelves union into the case; removable shelves print separately and rest on tapered ledges. Their narrower width clears the front opening. One near half height, two near thirds, or up to six explicit underside heights measured from the complete assembled floor. Suggested positions move away from caps, cable exits and connector centres; custom positions fail visibly rather than move silently. Live measurements show actual heights and clear space above each platform.
- Up to two removable sorting fins per floor/shelf. Their selected 20–120 mm height reduces automatically beneath a shelf/top, leaving 1.2 mm. Paired raised guides locate each fin. Lift 3.2 mm before pulling forward. Fins print broad-side down; shelves print flat; case additions retain the back-down case orientation.
- Optional back cable exits (bottom/every module, 6–16 mm), wall ventilation (honeycomb, diamond, short slots; back/sides/both, 6–12 mm). Solid edge frames, shelf support bands and rear port keepouts remain. Floors stay solid.
- Cupboard labels on the pull panel, one per column; drawer labels per drawer. Engraved text or recessed holders with separate 0.8 mm inserts. Holders accept paper or printed inserts retained by removable adhesive. One text line repeats; multiple lines follow column order, and bottom-row-first order for drawers. Shorten labels if the font would be under 4 mm tall. Drawer pads replace the local surface/openwork and retain clearance to optional colour sheets.

No process settings changed: A1 mini/0.4 mm/PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 8% gyroid, 2 wall loops, 3/3 skins with zero minimum skin thickness. Thin labels are naturally solid where skin regions overlap. Weight estimates and downloads include the added printable parts.

Validation: `test/cupboard-options.test.js` covers whole-stack positions, explicit rejection, connected meshes, limits including 70 mm-wide columns, closed/open-door collision checks, forward removal, vents/cables, labels and drawer colour sheets. `scripts/check-storage-options-browser.mjs` covers actual downloads, mobile, reset and validation. `scripts/check-storage-options-slicer.mjs` imports and slices representative cases, door, shelf, fin and label in official Bambu Studio 2.8.2.61. Desktop Open Project / Save Project also retained profiles for actual cupboard/drawer downloads. Native slicing completed; the headless runtime logs display initialization warnings and occasional internal ZFiller diagnostics, so CLI success does not prove physical print quality. Shelf loads, adhesive retention, clearance, ventilation bridges and assembled stability still require physical testing.

M5 shelf clearance: removable shelves/fins are offset away from the hinged side to pass the open brackets. Fixed shelves have a local hinge-corner relief. Shelf-guide rails stop 4 mm short of the rear to clear connector heads; removable shelf heights avoid the stationary hinge leaves. Case mesh cleanup discards only disconnected fragments below 0.001 mm³ from Boolean/simplification round-off.
