# Pets, Photography and Reading collections

Twenty-nine browser-local products (resizable accessories plus the original fixed-size Circa calendar) share the existing product editor, actual-mesh preview, printer picker, filament estimates, STL ZIP and complete Bambu 3MF exporter. The homepage and grouped navigation link to three new pages. Default printer: A1 mini, 170 mm finished-part allowance; optional Bambu A1, 256 mm. Each part is checked in its supplied printing pose and multi-part products receive prepared plates.

Six geometric surface choices are shared by suitable pots, cases and display pieces. Preview body/detail colours follow the existing palette selector. They do not create multi-filament print assignments. Notebook covers have smooth or finely grooved outside surfaces. No bookends are included.

Ordinary products retain 8% gyroid, 2 walls and 3 top/3 bottom layers. The lead rack, bowl riser and open book stand use `isLoadBearingPrint` and the existing reinforced 30% gyroid, 4 walls and 5/5 policy. Lithophane, fan, coaster and other existing profiles are unchanged.

Lids use a 3.2 mm inset collar with selectable per-side clearance; their flat outside face is placed on the bed. The bag-roll envelope includes axial space below this collar and a strap eye is integral. The manual turntable rests on the base face; its spindle has radial clearance and 1 mm axial clearance above the bore floor, and prints upward from the platter face. No separate pins are required. Hooks print wall-face-down with rising tip ramps; the book stand prints base-down with rounded triangular frame supports.

The picture frame has a front-down bezel, removable back, four captive turn buttons and a cradle. Supply four M3 × 6 mm screws and M3 hex nuts. Rear nut pockets and blind screw holes leave the front face intact. Stepped pressure pads touch the back while button arms clear the frame body. The frame accepts unglazed pictures up to 0.4 mm thick.

Notebook holes sit 12 mm from the left cover edge, corresponding to 6 mm inside centred paper. First/last holes sit 15 mm from cover ends, corresponding to 10 mm inside paper ends. Use standard loose-leaf binder rings and punch to the generated three/six-hole layout. Paper and rings are not printed. The preview shows the covers open, side by side, and labels that displayed size accordingly.

Treat storage is for a removable food-safe bag/liner. Wall rack fixings must match the wall. The rack stores leads rather than tethering an animal. General assembly, materials/use limits and hardware requirements are in each download guide.

Validation covers every part's positive closed connected mesh, both printer limits, large A1 examples, mating component intersections/clearances, correct notebook paper punch locations, invalid layouts, distinct surface styles and shared print profiles. Browser checks cover each editor, actual resize and palette changes, both printer downloads, 390/320 px viewports, stale exports and reset. Actual Bambu import/slicing audits all exported objects and plate membership; desktop Open/Save verifies preserved process/printer values on both printers. Gallery cards use screenshots of the actual model meshes.

## Fifteen additional products

The bowl riser supports an existing bowl by its rim, with clearances for the body and bottom, and prints ring-face-down. The scoop includes a hanging hole. Toy-basket finger openings extend through the top; narrow optional wall slots retain a solid floor. Tags offer oval, bone and rounded-rectangle outlines, a split-ring hole, and one line of fitted raised lettering from the locally bundled Noto Sans Bold font. Ball racks and cap racks show nonprinted reference objects, excluded from exports.

The card case has fixed nominal SD/microSD sizes, exposed card tops and a deep removable cover. Battery pockets accept rectangular or round cells without electrical contacts. Strip frames offer two to four windows and share the picture-frame hardware. Both frame types now use small rear-pocket corner radii so square paper corners fit. Riser sets print display-face-down.

The glasses tray and sticky-note holder retain solid floors below their finger scoops. Stencil ticks have true millimetre spacing and a defined zero tick. Paper trays have four integral stacking pegs and matching underside sockets, with a lowered front and full-height corners. Pen rests use two aligned open saddles. All additions retain the printer picker, dimensional validation and individual part exports.

Expansion checks cover nominal cards and covers, physical tray stacking, bowl clearance and rim overlap, cap saddles, all tag outlines and fitted lettering, square paper corners, round battery pockets, stencil spacing and invalid/large-printer layouts.

Circa retains the exact original geometry, four AMS slots and interchangeable stands. See `docs/circa-calendar.md` for source provenance, diagonal spine orientation and its support/prime-tower settings.
