# Pets, Photography and Reading collections

Thirteen browser-local, resizable products share the existing product editor, actual-mesh preview, printer picker, filament estimates, STL ZIP and complete Bambu 3MF exporter. The homepage and grouped navigation link to three new pages. Default printer: A1 mini, 170 mm finished-part allowance; optional Bambu A1, 256 mm. Each part is checked in its supplied printing pose and multi-part products receive prepared plates.

| Collection | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Pets | Lift-off treat pot, wall-mounted lead rack, refillable waste-bag dispenser, grooming organiser |
| Photography & display | Angled photo/card stand, interchangeable picture frame and cradle, tapered miniature plinth, hand-turned display stand |
| Reading & stationery | Plain/window bookmarks, thumb page holder, open book stand, ring-bound notebook covers, lift-off pen case |

Six geometric surface choices are shared by suitable pots, cases and display pieces. Preview body/detail colours follow the existing palette selector. They do not create multi-filament print assignments. Notebook covers have smooth or finely grooved outside surfaces. No bookends are included.

Ordinary products retain 8% gyroid, 2 walls and 3 top/3 bottom layers. The lead rack and open book stand use `isLoadBearingPrint` and the existing reinforced 30% gyroid, 4 walls and 5/5 policy. Lithophane, fan, coaster and other existing profiles are unchanged.

Lids use a 3.2 mm inset collar with selectable per-side clearance; their flat outside face is placed on the bed. The bag-roll envelope includes axial space below this collar and a strap eye is integral. The manual turntable rests on the base face; its spindle has radial clearance and 1 mm axial clearance above the bore floor, and prints upward from the platter face. No separate pins are required. Hooks print wall-face-down with rising tip ramps; the book stand prints base-down with rounded triangular frame supports.

The picture frame has a front-down bezel, removable back, four captive turn buttons and a cradle. Supply four M3 × 6 mm screws and M3 hex nuts. Rear nut pockets and blind screw holes leave the front face intact. Stepped pressure pads touch the back while button arms clear the frame body. The frame accepts unglazed pictures up to 0.4 mm thick.

Notebook holes sit 12 mm from the left cover edge, corresponding to 6 mm inside centred paper. First/last holes sit 15 mm from cover ends, corresponding to 10 mm inside paper ends. Use standard loose-leaf binder rings and punch to the generated three/six-hole layout. Paper and rings are not printed. The preview shows the covers open, side by side, and labels that displayed size accordingly.

Treat storage is for a removable food-safe bag/liner. Wall rack fixings must match the wall. The rack stores leads rather than tethering an animal. General assembly, materials/use limits and hardware requirements are in each download guide.

Validation covers every part's positive closed connected mesh, both printer limits, large A1 examples, mating component intersections/clearances, correct notebook paper punch locations, invalid layouts, distinct surface styles and shared print profiles. Browser checks cover each editor, actual resize and palette changes, both printer downloads, 390/320 px viewports, stale exports and reset. Actual Bambu import/slicing audits all exported objects and plate membership; desktop Open/Save verifies preserved process/printer values on both printers. Gallery cards use screenshots of the actual model meshes.
