FROM FIRST FIT TO FIRST CLICK

A few small parts. A lot of little clicks.

Check your switch

Dry-fit it in the square pocket. Its flange sits on the ledge around the pocket; its pins stay inside the closed base. Adjust pocket width and depth if needed.

Print base and caps

Print the base bottom-down. Print caps open-side-down with supports under the roof if needed; keep cross sockets clear.

Glue, cure, click

Glue the fixed switch housing to the pocket sides or ledge with small dabs of compatible adhesive. Keep stems and caps glue-free. Let it cure, fit the caps, then check full travel.
