Your Kitchen item

Surface styles are printed geometry. Preview colours do not assign filament. The same designs and styles are shared across Home & Living collections.

Size it to your space

Dimensions include surface detail. Parts are checked again in their printing orientation. Matching lids and connectors require the same size, wall, style and clearance settings.

Build your roll holder

28 mm shaft with right-handed screw joints. Each section stays within 150 mm; joints overlap when assembled. Check the usable shaft span below against your roll. Supply your own dry ballast for the weighted base; the loose cover does not seal it.

Match your cocktail sticks

Unscrew the lid to refill. Invert and gently shake to dispense. Choose an opening slightly larger than your sticks; this is a simple gravity dispenser.

Keep your soap in place

Raised sides are integral with the lift-out platform. Choose 0 for a flat platform. Assembled height includes these sides above the catch tray.

Let it drain

Catch trays stay solid. Empty and rinse them regularly. Test the printed tray for leaks on a protected surface.

Openwork

Solid frames remain around openings. Floor openings are capped at 12 mm for support. Small walls may have fewer or no cells. Inspect bridges and support needs in your slicer; supports start off.

Inside layout

Compartments resize automatically. Reduce rows or columns if the walls leave too little usable space.
