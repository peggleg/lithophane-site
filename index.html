02 / FROM IDEA TO FIRST PRINT

A few steps.
Something that’s yours.

01

Choose a project

Choose a lithophane, folding fan, nameplate, keyring, clicker or desk item. Bring a photo you love, or start with a few words.

02

Make it your own

Adjust photo tones or lettering, dimensions and preview colours in the generator. Check the preview before downloading.

03

Bring it to life

Download STL or 3MF files, open them in your slicer, and follow the project’s printing and assembly guide.

You’ll need a 3D printer and suitable filament. Defaults are 8% gyroid sparse infill and 2 wall loops. 3MF includes starting settings; confirm them in your slicer. Open the 3MF as a Bambu Studio project: A1 mini, 0.4 mm nozzle, Generic PLA and textured PEI plate. Select your actual material and plate; confirm settings after changing presets.

YOUR MEMORIES STAY YOURS
