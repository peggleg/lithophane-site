# Monstera coaster outline

The earlier shape was a rounded blade with three straight repeating slots per side. The revised original cubic drawing uses a pointed cordate blade, curved basal lobes, a short curved petiole, uneven rounded edge lobes and deep marginal splits. Seven teardrop-shaped inner fenestrations vary in size, position and angle.

Botanical reference: [University of Minnesota Extension — Monstera deliciosa](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/propagating-monstera-deliciosa), describing mature heart-shaped, perforated, deeply lobed leaves. No reference photograph is traced or shipped.

Both backing options share the lobed silhouette. The open version cuts the inner holes through; the backed version retains a continuous floor beneath them. Recessed coasters retain their perimeter rim. A shared transform normalizes the drawing to the exact requested width/depth; holders use that same outline. The profile remains 8% gyroid, 2 walls and 5/5 skin layers for coasters, 3/3 for holders.

Validation includes all surfaces, backing choices and 4–6 counts, holder insertion/rack clearances, actual part limits, all holder finishes, seven enclosed holes and a connected 0.8 mm inset core at 60 × 60, 60 × 160, 160 × 60, 100 × 100 and 160 × 160 mm. This verifies nominal geometry; printed fit and moisture resistance require physical tests.
