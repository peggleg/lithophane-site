03 / FROM PIXELS TO PRINT

Small details.
Better first prints.

These are starting points, not validated frame-specific profiles.

Filament & slicing

Start with white PLA, 0.12–0.16 mm layers, and enough walls or infill to make the panel solid. Mesh spacing controls image sampling, independently of nozzle width and layer height. Start with 0.2 mm spacing; 0.1 mm can retain finer source detail but creates larger files. Slice at 100% scale. Inspect the toolpaths for voids and thin areas. Check the dimensions before printing.

Orientation & adhesion

Try the panel standing vertically on its bottom edge, with a brim for stability. Fine relief benefits from slow outer walls. Use your slicer's estimate for print time and filament use.

Fit, lighting & safety

Print the corner test piece first. The 0.2 mm allowance is mechanical fit clearance, not thermal spacing. Keep LEDs away from the plastic, provide ventilation, follow the light manufacturer's guidance and test temperatures. Keep batteries secure and away from children. Use LED tealights only; never a flame. The postcard frame includes a cable exit; ensure the cable is not pinched.

Privacy & clearing your session

Photos are decoded and processed in your browser. This app does not upload photos, previews or models and uses no analytics, session replay, cloud storage, cookies. It stores only your colour-theme preference locally; photos and models are never saved to local storage. Reset or close this tab to release the active session. Your browser may retain memory temporarily; downloads and the original photo remain on your device until you delete them. MakerWorld is a separate external website.

Photo rights & model licensing

Use photos you own or have permission to reproduce. Your generated panel is downloaded directly to your device. Frame designs have their own licences on MakerWorld; check each licence before sharing, remixing or selling prints.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your first print.

What is a lithophane?

A lithophane is a thin relief panel that reveals an image when light shines through it. Dark photo areas become thicker plastic; light areas become thinner.

Is the lithophane maker free?

Yes. Create and download custom-sized STL or 3MF panels without payment or an account. You provide the printer, filament and lighting.

Do my photos leave my device?

No. Photo processing, preview generation and model exports happen in your browser. This app does not upload or store your photos or generated files. Downloads are saved by your browser on your device.

Can I print this with a Bambu Lab printer?

You can open the STL or 3MF geometry in your slicer and choose a suitable printer profile. Exports do not contain printer-specific settings. The postcard frame for the Bambu LED kit is available in the Frames section.

Where can I download the frames?

Download the postcard frame for the Bambu LED kit on MakerWorld. Maximum panel size: 105 × 148 × 3 mm, portrait or landscape.
