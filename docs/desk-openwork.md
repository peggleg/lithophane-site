# Desk openwork

Surface style (renamed from Japandi finish) remains independent of Openwork pattern. Patterns: honeycomb, diamond lattice, vertical slots, rounded windows, arch windows and woven grid. Woven grid includes alternating tall/wide apertures and 0.4 mm raised strips. Opening widths are adjustable from 4–20 mm. Drawer fronts use a centred field extending to a slim rounded border: partial edge cells are trimmed to that border and the actual pull surround. Diamonds form a staggered lattice; slots and arches span the panel height. Organiser walls retain complete cells.

Placement is front only or front and both sides. Applies to removable drawers, square pen pots, sorting-tray exteriors and standalone sorting/pen trays. Drawer cases and removable inner boxes are unaffected; round pots and swing-lid bins keep solid walls and display that restriction. Floors, back walls, corner frames, top rails and pull surrounds stay solid. On organiser walls, complete cells are omitted if they cannot fit inside the frame; narrow/low walls can have no cells. Minimum connecting web is 2.4 mm. Drawer fronts use a border at least 2.4 mm or wall thickness, with only the local finger-notch outline protected. Organiser frames remain at least 4 mm or twice wall thickness, with 9 mm corner clearance. Woven side relief is included when reserving drawer clearance and finished footprints. Every actual exported part remains checked against the 170 mm design limit.

The existing shared print profile and its desktop override list are unchanged: 8% gyroid, 2 wall loops, desk 3/3 top/bottom layers, zero minimum shell thickness. Supports remain off in the starting project; guides ask users to inspect bridging/support needs. Physical strength and print quality are not established by mesh or slicing checks.
