03 / FROM IDEA TO FIRST PRINT

A few steps.
Something that’s yours.

01

Choose a project

Choose a photo project, a personal keepsake, or something for your desk, kitchen or bathroom. Start with an idea and make it fit your space.

02

Make it your own

Adjust photos, lettering, dimensions and surface styles in the generator. Check the preview before downloading.

03

Bring it to life

Download STL or 3MF files, open them in your slicer, and follow the project’s printing and assembly guide.

You’ll need a 3D printer and suitable filament. Defaults are 8% gyroid sparse infill and 2 wall loops. 3MF includes starting settings; confirm them in your slicer. Open the 3MF as a Bambu Studio project: your selected printer (A1 mini by default), 0.4 mm nozzle, Generic PLA and textured PEI plate. Select your actual material and plate; confirm settings after changing presets.
