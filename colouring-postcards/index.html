Colouring postcards

Pick a funny character, print the white postcard with raised black outlines, then bring it to life with acrylic markers.

Portrait: 105 × 148 mm. Landscape: 148 × 105 mm. White base: 1.6–2.4 mm. Raised black lines: 0.6 mm.

Print face up at 0.2 mm layer height. For a manual colour change, switch from white to black at 2.4 mm, before the first raised line layer. The AMS project assigns both colours.
