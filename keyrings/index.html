Your words

Up to 60 characters in total. Latin letters, available accents, numbers and punctuation. Text fits down automatically; fitted font size must remain at least 5 mm.

Tag design

Changing shape starts with proportions that suit it. You can then adjust the dimensions. The ring hole sits on the left for rectangles and capsules, and at the top for other shapes, with at least 3 mm of surrounding material. Hearts use an upper lobe. Supply the metal ring separately.

Add a little character

Drag a symbol with mouse or touch. Select it and use arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps). Resize below. We keep symbols inside the tag and away from the ring hole.

Printing, fit and fonts

Start with a 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.2 mm layers, flat backs on the bed. Inspect letter counters, dots and accents in the slicer. Recessed text leaves at least 1.2 mm of base. Print quality, ring-hole strength and small details require physical testing.

STL contains the keyring as one solid. 3MF contains the same keyring geometry in millimetres. Download the print guide separately. Supply the metal ring yourself.
