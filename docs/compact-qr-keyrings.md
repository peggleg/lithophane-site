# Compact QR keyrings

Enabling QR mode now sizes a rounded tag from the encoded matrix and ring-hole diameter. The exact `https://example.com` payload starts at 42 × 35 mm rather than 65 × 60 mm. The fit button reapplies compact sizing; manual dimension edits stop automatic sizing until it is reapplied. Other decorative outlines retain their larger shape presets.

The encoder and payload are unchanged, including M error correction, the full four-module quiet border and a minimum 0.8 mm module for a 0.4 mm nozzle. Existing 3 mm edge and ring-hole protection remain. Longer content gets a larger compact tag. This reduces the tag footprint rather than deleting the required blank scan border. Digital decoding is not a physical scan guarantee; test contrasting filament and scan the printed tag.

Validation covers decoded relief meshes for the example link, punctuation/UTF-8, longer links and the 120-byte limit with 3/5/8 mm ring holes, plus browser auto-sizing, manual sizing, fit button, mobile and both printer exports. Native Bambu import and desktop Open/Save checks preserve 8% gyroid, two walls and 2/2 skins on both printers.
